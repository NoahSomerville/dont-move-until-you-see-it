Don't Move Until You See It

A puzzle flashes on the board for a few seconds, then the pieces vanish. Solve it from memory: tap the square your piece starts on, then the square it goes to.

For mate in 2, after your first move you'll be told your opponent's reply — then find the mate, still without seeing the board.

Any move that checkmates counts, not just the "book" answer.
